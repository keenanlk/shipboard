What it does

One quiet light in your menu bar that means everything's shipping.

ShipBoard polls your pipelines in the background and surfaces only what matters — so you can stop keeping five dashboards open.

Five providers, one board

AWS CodePipeline, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Vercel, and Netlify — grouped by account, added in a couple of clicks.

Live status at a glance

Every stage shows green, amber, or red in real time, and the menu-bar icon flags a failure the second it happens.

Notified the moment it breaks

Native macOS notifications when a pipeline fails — or recovers. Toggle success and failure pings separately.

Your credentials never leave your Mac

AWS uses your local ~/.aws profiles, read-only — SSO or access keys, your choice. Provider tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain and verified before they're saved. ShipBoard talks only to the providers themselves; nothing is sent anywhere else.

Set your own pace

Pick a refresh interval from 15 seconds to 5 minutes — and it polls faster while something's failing.

Stop babysitting dashboards.

Install ShipBoard, point it at your pipelines, and forget about it — until it lights up red.
